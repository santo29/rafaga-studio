# Code of Conduct · Código de conducta

Ráfaga Studio is built by and for creators from everywhere. We want this community to be welcoming and useful.

## Expected · Lo que esperamos

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners and with people writing in their second language.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Credit other people's work, and only share content you own or have permission to use.

## Not accepted · Lo que no aceptamos

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats of any kind.
- Sexual content, and anything that involves minors in an inappropriate way.
- Sharing other people's private information, or deepfakes of real people without their consent.
- Spam, scams and self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement · Cómo se aplica

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules.
To report a problem, use the channels in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#contacto). Reports are handled confidentially.
